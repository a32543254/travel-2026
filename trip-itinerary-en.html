🇹🇷 ISTANBUL — Days 1–3 (Sep 22–24)

Stay: Renaissance Istanbul Polat Bosphorus Hotel　Car: Renault Clio

Day 1 | Sep 22 (Tue) — Arrival

  • 16:27 arrive at IST → hotel transfer is about 45–60 minutes. Pick up the car, but do not drive into the city center on day one.
  • Evening: Walk around Ortaköy Square and enjoy the mosque + Bosphorus Bridge at night. Try kumpir and waffles.
  • Dinner: Tugra (reservation required) or Ortaköy Beyaz Fırın.
  • Tip: Get an eSIM/Turkcell SIM and buy an Istanbulkart.

Day 2 | Sep 23 (Wed) — Old City Highlights

Morning 08:30–12:00 | Sultanahmet

  • Hagia Sophia — book online in advance with a timed slot; the upper Gallery needs a separate ticket.
  • Blue Mosque — free, but closed during prayer times. Women need a headscarf; loaners are available.

Lunch: Sultanahmet Köftecisi Selim Usta or Deraliye Ottoman Cuisine.

Afternoon 13:30–17:30

  • Topkapı Palace — book ahead; the Harem is a separate ticket.
  • Basilica Cistern — timed ticket strongly recommended.
  • Grand Bazaar (closed Sundays) + Spice Bazaar.

Evening: Bosphorus sunset cruise (book the day before).

Dinner: Karaköy Lokantası (reservation needed) or Balıkçı Sabahattin.

Optional experience: Traditional hammam — advance booking essential.

Day 3 | Sep 24 (Thu) — Half Day, then fly to Chisinau

  • Flight departs 13:40 → arrive at the airport by 11:00, so the morning is limited.
  • 07:30–09:30: Galata Tower exterior / pre-booked entry, or İstiklal Avenue + nostalgic tram; breakfast at Van Kahvaltı Evi.
  • 09:30: Return the car and head to the airport.
  • IST is huge — allow at least 2.5 hours for check-in, security, and passport control.

🇲🇩 CHISINAU — Days 3–8 (Sep 24–29)

Stay: ASTORIA Boutique Hotel　Car: Porsche Macan

Day 3 | Sep 24 — Evening arrival

  • Arrive at 15:20, pick up the car, check in. Take an evening walk along Ștefan cel Mare Boulevard and Central Park.
  • Dinner: Vatra Neamului — try mămăligă, brânză, and plăcinte.

Day 4 | Sep 25 (Fri) — City Day

  • Morning: Triumphal Arch, Cathedral Park, National History Museum, Central Market.
  • Lunch: Gustos or La Plăcinte.
  • Afternoon: Cricova Winery — pre-book an English-language tour slot; use a designated driver if tasting.
  • Dinner: Carbon or Propaganda Cafe.

Day 5 | Sep 26 (Sat) — Winery Day Trip

  • Milestii Mici (world’s largest wine cellar) + Château Purcari. Both require advance booking.
  • Moldova has zero-tolerance drink-driving laws — hire a driver for tasting days.

Day 6 | Sep 27 (Sun) — Monasteries & Countryside

  • Orheiul Vechi + Butuceni village.
  • Lunch: Casa din Luncă or Vila Roz (reserve on weekends).
  • On the way back, stop at Curchi Monastery.
  • Dinner: Taraf (live folk music available).

Day 7 | Sep 28 (Mon) — Choose one

  • Option 1: Tiraspol / Transnistria day trip — border registration required; bring your passport and do not photograph military/border facilities.
  • Option 2: Castel Mimi winery (reservation recommended) + city shopping.
  • Dinner: Popasul Dacilor or near the hotel.

Day 8 | Sep 29 (Tue) — Fly to Rome

  • Flight at 09:35 → arrive at the airport by 07:00. Refuel and prep the car return the night before.

🇮🇹 ROME — Days 8–10 (Sep 29 – Oct 1)

Stay: Hero's Garden Relais　Car: Alfa Romeo Tonale

Day 8 | Sep 29 — Arrival

  • Afternoon: Walk around Trastevere. Lunch/dinner at Roscioli or Da Enzo al 29 (reservation essential).
  • Evening: Trevi Fountain + Pantheon (ticketed, buy online) + Spanish Steps at night.
  • Rome has ZTL restricted zones — do not drive into the city center; park at the hotel or in a garage.

Day 9 | Sep 30 (Wed) — Ancient Rome, Full Day

  • 08:00: Colosseum + Roman Forum + Palatine Hill combined ticket — must be booked on the official platform with a timed slot. Arena / Underground requires special tickets.
  • Lunch: La Carbonara or Ai Tre Scalini.
  • Afternoon: Capitoline Museums, or Galleria Borghese (mandatory reservation, strict 2-hour window).
  • Evening: aperitivo at Campo de' Fiori or Piazza Navona.
  • Dinner: Armando al Pantheon (very hard to book).

Day 10 | Oct 1 (Thu) — Vatican + Drive to Naples

  • 07:30–11:30: Vatican Museums + Sistine Chapel — timed ticket required; St. Peter’s Basilica is free but lines are long, and the dome climb is a separate ticket.
  • Dress code: no bare shoulders, shorts must cover the knee.
  • After lunch, collect the car and drive south on the A1 motorway to Naples.

🇮🇹 NAPLES — Days 10–12 (Oct 1–3)

Stay: Terrazza Monteroduni

Day 10 | Oct 1 — Evening arrival

  • Dinner: L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele, 50 Kalò, or Sorbillo.
  • Naples driving and parking are chaotic — keep the car in a paid supervised garage.

Day 11 | Oct 2 (Fri) — Pompeii + City

  • Morning: Pompeii — buy tickets online and hire a guide or audio guide.
  • Lunch: Ristorante President (reservation needed) or something quick.
  • Afternoon: Naples National Archaeological Museum.
  • Evening: Spaccanapoli, Via San Gregorio Armeno, and Cappella Sansevero for the Veiled Christ (timed tickets must be booked ahead).
  • Dinner: Trattoria da Nennella or Tandem.

Day 12 | Oct 3 (Sat) — To Capri

  • Morning: Castel dell'Ovo / Lungomare.
  • Ferry to Capri:
  • Cars are not allowed on the island; park the car at a long-stay garage at Naples port.
  • Book ferry tickets ahead because sailings may be canceled in rough seas.

🏝️ CAPRI — Days 12–14 (Oct 3–5)

Stay: Hotel Weber Ambassador

Day 12 | Oct 3 — Afternoon arrival

  • Take the funicular or bus to Capri town. Walk Via Camerelle and Giardini di Augusto to see the Faraglioni rocks and Via Krupp.
  • Sunset aperitivo on the hotel terrace or at Il Riccio.

Day 13 | Oct 4 (Sun) — Full Island Day

  • Morning: Blue Grotto (Grotta Azzurra) — depends on sea conditions and tide; best booked as a half-day boat tour in advance.
  • Lunch: Da Gioia or Lo Scoglio delle Sirene.
  • Afternoon: Anacapri + Monte Solaro chairlift + Villa San Michele.
  • Dinner: Il Cucciolo or Aurora (reservation recommended).

Day 14 | Oct 5 (Mon) — To Positano

  • Free morning.
  • Ferry back to Naples → pick up the car → drive to Positano.
  • The Amalfi Coast road is narrow and winding; drive carefully, especially at night or in rain.

🌊 POSITANO — Days 14–17 (Oct 5–8)

Stay: Casa Salvi Positano

Day 14 | Oct 5 — Evening arrival

  • Parking is very difficult in Positano — confirm a spot or pre-book a garage.
  • Evening: Spiaggia Grande and Santa Maria Assunta church.
  • Dinner: Chez Black or La Cambusa.

Day 15 | Oct 6 (Tue) — Amalfi + Ravello

  • Morning: take the SITA bus or ferry to Amalfi.
  • Lunch: Da Gemma or Marina Grande.
  • Afternoon: Ravello — Villa Rufolo and Villa Cimbrone.
  • Dinner: back in Positano at Next2 or Il Ritrovo.

Day 16 | Oct 7 (Wed) — Experience Day

  • ① Path of the Gods
  • ② Private boat tour of the Amalfi Coast
  • ③ Cooking class or lemon-grove experience
  • Evening: sunset drinks at Da Ferdinando, or farewell dinner at La Sponda.

Day 17 | Oct 8 (Thu) — Departure

  • Flight at 22:40 from FCO.
  • Suggested plan: leave Positano around 10:00, drive to Rome (~3.5h), return the car by 14:00, then check in around 15:00.
  • Refuel before returning the car and take photos as proof.

⚠️ Trip-Wide Notes

  1. Renting cars in Italy, Moldova, and Türkiye requires an International Driving Permit (1968 Convention version) + your original national license.
  2. Rome, Naples, and Amalfi towns all have ZTL restricted zones.
  3. Confirm visa requirements for Türkiye, Moldova, and Schengen separately.
  4. Bring a light jacket for windy boat rides.
  5. Capri and Positano have lots of stairs, so big suitcases are inconvenient.
  6. Watch for pickpockets in Rome Termini, Naples station, and Istanbul Grand Bazaar.
  7. Cash is still useful in Moldova and in some Italian situations.
